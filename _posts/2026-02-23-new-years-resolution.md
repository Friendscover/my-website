The beginning of a new year always feels like a new start to a lot of people, including myself. The majority might set new goals for the new year. But it almost feels like most of these goals stay hidden or won’t be reached for in the following months. I felt the same way in the previous years and this year might be the same. Especially since I am writing this posts at the end of february.

### This years goals
I have set a few goals for my personal development in 2026, but I also wanna do a few updates to my blog. These are my goals for 2026:
- reading atleast 10 books
- listen to atleast 20 albums of new artist
- write and publish 10 blogs posts (this one is ambitious)

My personal goals are focussed on less media consumption and lean towards a more intentional consumption of entertainment. I don’t approve of the curent state of social media doom scrolling and these goals are one step away from that. We will see how that’s working in a few months.

### Minor updates to the blog
In the last year I have been doing some minor updates to the blog. I was deleting some links to social media platforms and overall reducing the complexity of the website. I have been inspired by some websites of the old web and I want to include a better landing page as the entry point for my blog. I would like to continue these small updates for 2026, which may include small or bigger css rewrites and reducing the complexity even further.

### Migrating to codeberg pages
The current blog is hosted via github pages but due to recent events involving Github, I am considering the migration to codeberg pages. The current codeberg pages implementation does not support custom domain names. Therefore I am awaiting on further development progress to make the switch.
